# How-to

Pages for things a caller sets out to do.

- [Walk a span of observations](walk-observations.md): every observation one station
  reported over a span of up to a week, all at once or one response at a time, with
  `stations.get_observations_paged`.

- [Walk alerts with a cursor](walk-alerts.md): decode the next cursor, preserve filters,
  and continue through short non-empty pages.
